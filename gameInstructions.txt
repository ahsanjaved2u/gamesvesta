///////////////////////////////////bubble crush////////////////////////////////////////

🍬 Bubble Crush
Swap candies to match 3 or more — chain combos for big points.

🎯 Match  3 = 3 pts • 4 = 5 pts • 5 = 10 pts • 6+ = 10 + 2 per extra
🔗 Cascade  Auto-chains after a match multiply your points
🚫 Penalty  Bad swap = -1 pt + strength drain
⚡ Strength  Drains on bad swaps — game over at 0
⏱️ Speed  Less time = more score
🏆 Rewards  Best daily score • Score → PKR

🚀 Space Shooter
Survive enemy waves, upgrade your ship, and rack up the highest score.

🎯 Kill  Red Core 1 • Spacecraft 2 • Drone 3 • Dangerous 5 pts
💎 Gem   Collect after kill for bonus pts
⚡ Strength  Green drop +10 • Blue drop +5 (game over at 0)
🛡️ Shield  Upgrades ship for 15 s
🚀 Ship   Every 5 pts = next tier (5 tiers)
⏱️ Speed  Less time = more score
🏆 Rewards  Best daily score • Score → PKR

///////////////////////////////////sword runner////////////////////////////////////////
⚔️ Sword Runner
Run the 3-lane valley — slash coins, dodge foes and barriers at ever-rising speed.

🎮 Controls  Swipe or A / D = change lane • Swipe up / tap = jump • Swipe down = roll
🪙 Coins  Bronze +1 • Silver +2 • Gold +3
🚧 Obstacles  Jump red barriers • Roll under bars • Dodge purple crystals
👾 Foes  Orange imp -1 ⚡ • Purple brute -2 ⚡ (jump imps, dodge brutes)
✖️ Multiplier  Overtake foes = score multiplier up
🛡️ Shield  Rare — appears only when hurt, refills strength
⚡ Strength  Every hit drains it — game over at 0
🌗 Theme  Day / Night toggle on the start card (T)
⏱️ Speed  Less time = more score
🏆 Rewards  Best daily score • Score → PKR
